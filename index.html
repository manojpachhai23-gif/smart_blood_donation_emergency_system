Frequently Asked Questions
How long does blood donation take?

About 45–60 minutes total, including registration, health screening, the donation itself (8–10 min), and a short rest.

Is donating blood safe?

Completely safe. Only sterile, single-use equipment is used. You cannot contract any disease by donating blood.

What are the eligibility requirements?

You must be at least 18, weigh at least 50 kg, be in good health, and meet other criteria checked during screening.
